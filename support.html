Why We Greet · Lester Barnett

He recalled a couple who wanted to have a glass of wine at each of a series of New York bars. “They wanted a rich bar, they wanted a poor bar, they wanted an Irish bar,” he said. Toward the end of the night, the woman went to the ladies’ room and her husband told Barnett that she had a terminal illness. “All she wanted to do was go to New York and toast each other all over the city,” Barnett said. “That’s why I’m a Greeter. You enter people’s lives in a way that is astounding.” As published in The New Yorker

Why Give

  • Small nonprofit, worldwide impact

    Big Apple Greeter has been welcoming the world since 1992: over 150,000 visitors, guided by New Yorkers through the neighborhoods of all five boroughs. A gift here is an investment in the city’s economy, its communities, and its people.

  • Tourism, spread wider

    Your gift promotes tourism across the whole city and sends visitors beyond the postcard stops, into lesser-known neighborhoods that are every bit as compelling.

  • First days, made easy

    Donations put Greeters beside first-time visitors: orienting them, decoding the subway, sharing favorite corners, and turning day one in New York into a good story instead of a hard one.

  • A friendlier New York, worldwide

    Every greet helps position New York City as a friendly, inviting, and manageable place to visit, and that reputation travels home with every guest.

  • Civic pride, activated

    Your support creates volunteer opportunities for New Yorkers to do what they already do naturally: show off where they live.

Monthly Giving · The Big Apple CORE

Join the CORE →
  • What it is

    A very special community of New Yorkers and friends of New York whose steady monthly gifts keep the welcome running all year long.

  • What you get

    A Certificate of Appreciation, a spot in our Meet Our CORE Members gallery, and your own volunteer concierge: Cheryl Fleisher, at cfleisher@bigapplegreeter.org or 212-669-7583.

  • What we get

    Predictable support with lower administrative costs, which means more of your gift goes straight to the mission.

  • On your terms

    Choose the 1st or 15th of the month, adjust your amount or billing anytime through your own donor account, and cancel whenever you need to. Payments are encrypted and secure, and you’ll get one tidy year-end tax receipt.

  • The company you’d keep

    CORE members like Jerry Rumain, Karen Tenser, Danielle LeCompte, and Allen Weingast are already holding the door open.
