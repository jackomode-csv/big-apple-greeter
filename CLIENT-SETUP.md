# Editing Your Website with Claude — Getting Started

You can update your website by describing changes in plain English. No code,
no special skills. Claude reads your words, makes the edit, and shows you what
changed before anything is saved.

## One-time setup (about 15 minutes)

1. **Get a Claude subscription** at claude.ai — the Pro plan is enough.
2. **Install the Claude Code desktop app** (Mac or Windows) from
   claude.com/claude-code — or use the web version at claude.ai/code.
3. **Open your website folder** in the app: File → Open Folder, then choose
   the "big-apple-greeter" folder Jack gave you.
4. That's it. The chat box at the bottom is where you type.

## How to ask for changes

Just describe what you want, like you'd tell an assistant:

- "Replace the first review with this one: 'Best day of our trip!' from
  Maria G, Lisbon, March 2027."
- "Add a news item: the Daily News wrote about us in May, here's the link."
- "Update the board list: Jane Smith replaced Howard Becker."
- "Change the phone number on the Support page to 212-555-1234."
- "Fix the spelling of 'neighborhood' on the About page."

Claude will tell you what it's about to change and ask you to confirm.
Say yes, and it's done.

## Publishing

After a change is saved, say **"publish it"** and the live site updates in
about a minute. If Claude says it can't publish, the change is still saved —
just let Jack know and he'll put it live.

## If something goes wrong

Say **"undo that last change"** — every change is saved as a snapshot, so
nothing is ever lost for good. If the site ever looks wrong and you're not
sure why, stop and call Jack rather than trying to fix it yourself.

## What not to ask Claude to do alone

Anything about the videos, animations, forms, or the map — those are wired
together carefully. Claude knows to be careful, but loop Jack in for those.
