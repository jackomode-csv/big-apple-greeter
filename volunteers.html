How We Do Things Around Here

  • Your neighborhood, your rules

    You take up to six guests on an informal, unscripted walk through the streets you know cold. No script, no flag to hold, no memorized dates.

  • Your schedule

    We pair you with visitors based on when you’re free, plus your language, neighborhoods, and interests. Greet once a month or once a week. Family shows up when it can.

  • Your spots

    The hidden places New Yorkers love and the tourist books miss. You share them like you would with a cousin in town. Yes, even that one.

  • Any borough

    All five, proudly. The more corners of the city the family covers, the better.

  • Any language

    English is all you need. A second language is a superpower here, and a great excuse to use it.

  • What you get back

    A good walk, new friends from all over the world, and the particular joy of watching someone fall for your city. It makes the world a little smaller and happier.
